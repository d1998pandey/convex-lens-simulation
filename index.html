NCERT Class 10 Chapter 10: Light – Reflection and Refraction

A lens is a transparent medium bound by two surfaces, of which one or both surfaces are spherical. Convex lenses converge light rays and are called converging lenses, whereas concave lenses diverge light rays and are called diverging lenses.

Sign Convention for Lenses

We follow Cartesian Sign Conventions. Optical centre O is taken as the origin. All distances parallel to the principal axis are measured from O. Object distance u is always negative. Focal length of convex lens is positive, and for concave lens, it is negative.

CBSE Class 10 Board Practice Quiz

Q1. Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the size of the object?

Answer: At twice the focal length ($2F_1$), the image is formed at $2F_2$ with exact 1:1 scale and real-inverted nature.

Q2. A concave lens has a focal length of 15 cm. At what distance should the object be placed from the lens so that it forms an image at 10 cm from the lens?

Answer: Using $1/v - 1/u = 1/f$, with $f = -15$ cm and $v = -10$ cm, we get $u = -30$ cm.
